1. EXTORTION MINIGAME
---------------------
- Rebuilt the old vertical timing interaction as a circular clockwise pressure meter.
- Added hold-and-release mouse and Space controls with one attempt per interaction.
- Added continuous extra revolutions instead of stopping when the player overshoots.
- Later revolutions increase speed gradually, capped at 1.25x.
- Final meter outcome areas:
  - Red: no deal, no money, and no protection.
  - Gray: one-time dirty-cash payoff, but no protection or territory.
  - White: protection established with a 20% weekly tribute bonus.
  - Black: best result, protection established with a 50% weekly tribute bonus.
- Added a longer first-time tutorial presentation and a shorter repeat refresher.
- Added animated zone explanations and a sample sweep before player control begins.
- Simplified and reorganized the minigame layout.
- Enlarged the central dollar symbol and removed unnecessary meter text.
- Added a horizontal result bar mirroring the circular outcome zones.
- Added contextual owner dialogue for pending, failed, gray, white, and black outcomes.
- Removed the attempt counter and redundant Hold Pressure button.
- Added polished result, tutorial, owner-message, and meter animations.


2. PROTECTION / EXTORTION ORDERS
--------------------------------
- Street Punks can no longer freely extort arbitrary buildings.
- Street Punk extortion is restricted to the target of an active Soldier-issued order.
- Added repeatable Collect Protection orders for eligible expandable territory buildings.
- Protection-order frequency is limited to a randomized one or two orders per week.
- Prevented overlapping active integrated orders.
- The target building is highlighted and tracked by the Active Order HUD.
- Successful protection requires returning to the Family Estate to report.
- Estate report actions now automatically route the player to the Estate when necessary.
- Reporting completes automatically after the player physically arrives.
- Mission rewards, XP, standing, superior trust, career records, and memories update on completion.
- Failed, refused, expired, active, and completed order states are retained on the original order.


3. NEW SCOUT LOCATION MISSION
-----------------------------
- Added a repeatable Soldier-issued Scout Location order for Street Punks.
- Missions select an active, valid building in the safehouse city area.
- Required observation duration is randomly selected from 6, 12, or 24 hours.
- Mission rewards scale with the required observation duration.
- Orders include acceptance and completion deadlines.
- The target building receives a mission highlight and Jump to Location support.
- The player must physically travel to the building and start Scout from its context menu.
- Scouting runs as a timed live-world action and updates the Active Order HUD.
- On completion, the mission changes to the return-and-report stage without creating Inbox spam.
- Route to Estate automatically walks the player there and files the report on arrival.
- Completion grants dirty cash, XP, standing, superior trust, career progress, and a memory.
- Mission generation is checked during moving-time ticks so new work can interrupt simulation.


4. BUILDING SCOUTING THRESHOLDS
-------------------------------
- Reduced normal building scouting thresholds to:
  - Structure information: 6 hours.
  - Usage information: 12 hours.
  - Valuation/full report: 24 hours.
- Updated scouting progress calculations and completion caps to 24 hours.
- Updated appraisal requirements and locked-tab instructions to use the new thresholds.
- Building intelligence gained during Scout Location missions contributes to normal scouting progress.

- Unread messages retain their warm color, shine, and stronger text hierarchy.
- Selected messages remain bright and receive a strong yellow outline even after being read.
- Read order messages stay brighter than ordinary read correspondence.
- Added order-state symbols:
  - Yellow ! for unaccepted orders.
  - Yellow rotating-arrow symbol for accepted/in-progress orders.
  - Red X for failed, refused, or expired orders.
  - Muted check mark for completed orders.
- Active orders retain an unread-like bright appearance until completed.
- Failed missions automatically become read.
- Incoming order messages pause the simulation and select the new Inbox entry.
- Only newly issued orders move the player toward Inbox.
- Mission-stage updates no longer create additional Inbox messages.
- Target sighted, return to report, protection established, scouting complete, report accepted,
  report rejected, and similar mission-stage correspondence is suppressed.
- Existing obsolete mission-stage messages are removed from the Inbox.
- Mission instructions and stage changes now remain in the live Active Order panel.

13. NEW ALCOHOL DEALING ORDER
-----------------------------
- Added a repeatable early-game Alcohol Dealing order issued through the existing Inbox system.
- Supplier locations are selected outside Mafia territory and biased toward distant valid buildings.
- Ordinary alcohol uses warehouses, industrial buildings, workshops, factories, and storage sites.
- Medicinal Whiskey uses hospitals, clinics, medical offices, doctors, or suitable civic buildings.
- Orders include acceptance deadlines, completion deadlines, issuer information, rewards,
  target highlighting, Jump to Location, and live Active Order instructions.
- The target does not automatically move the player; the player must accept the order and travel.
- Failed supplier negotiations end the order without creating additional mission-stage messages.
- Successful purchases progress into truck loading, Estate delivery, unloading, and completion.
- Alcohol orders are limited to one per week and cannot overlap another active integrated order.
- Orders do not spawn when no affordable and rank-eligible shipment exists.


14. RANK-LOCKED ALCOHOL TYPES
-----------------------------
- Street Punk access:
  - Homemade Wine or Cider.
  - Bathtub Gin.
- Associate access:
  - Moonshine.
  - Medicinal Whiskey.
- Soldier access:
  - Bootlegged imported Scotch and Rye.
- Higher ranks retain access to lower-rank alcohol.
- Rare imported spirits can appear as loose bottles or exceptionally rare full crates.
- Shipment quantity, package type, price, volume, rarity, and negotiation difficulty are generated
  from the alcohol definition rather than being hardcoded into the mission flow.


15. MAFIA TREASURY AND PROCUREMENT RULES
----------------------------------------
- Alcohol belongs to the Mafia and is no longer purchased with player clean or dirty cash.
- Reused the existing state.organizationFinance.coffers ledger used by Estate envelope deliveries.
- Added a one-time $10,000 Providence testing grant to Mafia coffers.
- Alcohol quality is capital-gated as well as rank-gated:
  - Low coffers primarily support homemade alcohol.
  - Increasing capital unlocks Gin, Moonshine, Medicinal Whiskey, and imported spirits.
- A 10% safe-spend metric determines whether a shipment is financially responsible enough to spawn.
- Shipment generation sizes cargo so the maximum gray-deal cost remains within that metric.
- The 10% value is not directly handed to the player.
- Accepting an order reserves the exact maximum gray-deal price as a separate accountable
  Family cash envelope.
- The supplier is paid only from this mission envelope; general player money remains untouched.
- Purchase advances and supplier payments create entries in the Mafia organization ledger.
- If the deal or mission fails, all unspent advance money returns to Mafia coffers.
- On successful Estate delivery, remaining money is divided evenly:
  - 50% becomes a personal dirty-cash savings bonus for the player.
  - 50% is returned to Mafia coffers.
- The ordinary order-completion reward remains separate from the procurement savings bonus.

31. DIRECTIONAL STREETLIGHT SHADING
-----------------------------------
- Replaced the broad scalar brightness texture with a 1024px directional light field

- Lit materials now use world-space surface normals: façades, cars, and pedestrians
  brighten only on the side facing the lamp, while opposite faces remain dark.
- Reduced lamp radius and ground-glow size substantially; illumination fades before

- Included lamp posts in the nighttime material pass and added low-cost projected

- Removed the legacy additive ground-plane decals completely; the visible pool now
  comes from the road and sidewalk materials receiving directional light themselves.


32. REAL BULB LIGHTING AND POPULATION SHADOWS
----------------------------------------------
- Removed the directional texture field and projected circle-shadow approximation;
  street lighting no longer paints a light or shadow decal onto the ground.
- Added real warm omnidirectional lights at the physical bulb height. At perceptible camera detail,
  nearby lamps illuminate roads, sidewalks, building facades, lamp posts, vehicles,
  and pedestrians using their actual surface direction and distance from the source.
- Added a bounded camera-detail light pool so hundreds of emissive streetlamps remain
  viable without compiling hundreds of realtime lights into every city material.
- Added soft shadow maps to the lamps nearest visible moving people and vehicles.
  Pedestrians, cars, buildings, roads, sidewalks, and estate surfaces now use proper
  shadow caster/receiver flags instead of simulated flat silhouettes.
- Distant city zoom keeps emissive bulbs but omits sub-pixel light pools and shadows;
  close and street-level views activate the full dynamic lighting pass.


33. BATCHED STREETLIGHT CONTAMINATION

- Added smooth, density-weighted light-contamination ribbons along the roads. Streets

- Added batched warm facade washes to the building sides facing illuminated streets,

--------------------------------------------------

- Kept all facade and light groups batched; the new schedules do not create per-window

37. ARCHITECTURAL WINDOW VARIANTS AND INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------
- Added six deterministic building-level facade rhythms: regular grids, tall panes,
  broad panes, paired bays, triple bays, and horizontal window bands. Buildings stay
  internally organized while no longer sharing one universal window arrangement.
- Long facades continue adding columns based on their physical length, with grouped
  layouts preserving safe spacing so paired and triple panes do not overlap.
- Replaced bright unlit frame materials with dark, wall-tinted Lambert materials for
  window, storefront, door, service-door, and garage outlines.
- Replaced the four synchronized fading light batches with per-pane schedule data on
  two city-wide GPU batches. Every eligible pane now switches instantly at its own
  deterministic evening on-time and nightly off-time.
- Overnight windows are selected from panes that already switched on during evening
  and remain continuously active through midnight. This removes the 00:00 pop while
  keeping roughly four percent of the city illuminated until individual morning
  shutoff times after 06:00.
- The implementation adds no per-window scene objects, JavaScript timers, or realtime
  lights; schedule evaluation happens in the existing batched window shader.


38. ROLLING NIGHT WINDOWS AND CAMERA OCCLUSION
----------------------------------------------
- Brightened illuminated glass while keeping every pane within the same warm-yellow
  family. Each pane now carries a deterministic brightness value, producing subtle
  dim, normal, and brighter interiors without introducing additional meshes.
- Replaced the fixed overnight window set with a rolling GPU schedule. Candidate panes
  change independently at staggered intervals as households switch lights off and on.
- Overnight occupancy decreases gradually from roughly four percent around midnight
  toward one percent by 06:00. The rolling pool begins progressively before midnight
  and shuts down progressively after 06:00, avoiding synchronized population changes.
- Added close-camera building obstruction handling. At close zoom or low isometric
  angles, a camera-to-focus ray identifies only blocking building meshes and renders
  them as dark translucent silhouettes until the view clears.
- Occluded buildings retain their geometry, facade openings, and edge readability;
