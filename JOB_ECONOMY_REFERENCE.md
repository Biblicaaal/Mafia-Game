# Job and personal-economy reference

This is the editable source reference for the current 1931 population economy. Values are weekly income in dollars. The active data lives in `public/desk-don/world-population.js`; update both places when changing balance.

Business owners receive 135% of the top career salary. Their generated personal reserve is approximately 7–18 weeks of that income plus a wealth allowance. Ordinary workers receive approximately 1–7 weeks plus a smaller wealth allowance.

The exact Mafia weekly value is deterministic per character and partially weighted by performance. Personal cash is deterministic per character inside the rank band.
